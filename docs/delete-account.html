Delete your Workpad account

Open Workpad, choose Settings, and select Delete account. Confirm the warning to permanently delete the signed-in account.

Account deletion cancels an active Workpad Pro subscription and deletes cloud notes, authentication data, and Workpad account records. This cannot be undone.

What remains

Notes stored locally in Chrome remain on that device so you can export them. To remove those too, use Reset local data after deleting the account, or remove the extension and its site data.

If you cannot sign in

Email ChocolateMinds at ai@chocolateminds.com. You may be asked to verify control of the account email. Do not post account details publicly.
