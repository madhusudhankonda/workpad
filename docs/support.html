Workpad Support

For product bugs and feature requests, use the Workpad issue tracker. Search existing reports before opening a new one.

Keep secrets private. Never include note content, browsing data, authentication tokens, card numbers, or private billing details in a public issue.

Account and billing help

Email ChocolateMinds privately at ai@chocolateminds.com. Include only your account email and Stripe receipt or invoice identifier. You can manage or cancel a subscription in Workpad Settings → Manage billing.

Before reporting a problem

  • Reload Workpad from chrome://extensions.
  • Confirm the page is a normal website rather than a restricted Chrome page.
  • For AI problems, check that you are signed in, check your remaining credits, and note the error shown in Workpad.
  • Export a backup before resetting local data.
