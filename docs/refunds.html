Workpad Refund Policy

Effective 24 August 2026

Workpad Pro is billed monthly. You can cancel at any time through Settings → Manage billing; cancellation prevents the next renewal and normally leaves Pro active until the current paid period ends.

Refund requests

If you were charged after cancelling, charged more than once, or could not use a paid feature because of a verified Workpad service fault, email ChocolateMinds at ai@chocolateminds.com. Do not post payment or account details in a public issue.

Requests should include the account email, charge date, and Stripe invoice or receipt identifier. Never send a card number or API key. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method. Processing time depends on Stripe and your bank.

Statutory rights

This policy does not limit refund or cancellation rights provided by applicable consumer law.
